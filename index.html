Frequency Banks

Total Revenue

Headline revenue with its three fee engines. Component definitions vary by filer — trading revenue in particular splits across different XBRL concepts — so trend shape within a bank is more reliable than cross-bank levels.

Net Income

The bottom line and its per-share expression. Diluted EPS reflects buybacks as well as earnings, which is why the EPS trend can outrun net income.

Consumer Credit Quality

Three proxies read together: provisions (the forward-looking P&L charge), the allowance (the balance-sheet reserve), and net charge-offs (realized losses). Provisions rising ahead of charge-offs signals management expects deterioration; the reverse signals releases.
